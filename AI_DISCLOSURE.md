# Use of AI assistance

OpenAI ChatGPT assisted with manuscript drafting and revision, writing and debugging analysis code, searching and screening literature, and auditing numerical results. The source data were generated by the original experimental investigators (Gervais and colleagues); the independent Python reimplementation and derived figures do not constitute new wet-laboratory experiments. Tests, source-hash checks and two computational environments are described in the article. These checks do not constitute independent human peer review.

The named human author authorized the public repository release and journal submission and is responsible for the work as submitted. This assistance is not independent human peer review, and ChatGPT is not a coauthor.
